After the Last Stretch

Five sessions a week at most, two of them on the weekend. One heavy day holds every goal lift; the other four go to the bench, cardio and the dunk, and the cut takes care of the abs. Starts November 30, 2026 and runs to June 27, 2027.

The sessions

Four lifting sessions, a short jump block, and the cardio and joint blocks that ride along with them. T is the bench top set, set by the Nov 19 test.

H Heavy full body ~90 min with warmups · weekday · fed
  • Bike or sled, pancakes, shoulder warmup with band pull-aparts10 min
  • Bench pressT × 5, then 2 × 5 at T − 15
  • Trap bar deadlift, ramp beltless, belted top set1 × 3–5 at 300
  • Pullups2 × 5
  • Smith squat, ramp, belted top set1 × 3–5 at 225
  • Dips1 × 10

Bench goes first because it's the only lift here meant to climb. Pullups sit between the two lower lifts so each half rests while the other works. Ramps aren't logged. The last heavy day of each block is the test: 5 reps at 300 and 225, and a max set of pullups.

B2 Bench volume 40 min · weekday
  • Bench press3 × 8 at T − 30
  • Incline chest press machine2 × 8
  • Cable row2 × 5
  • Cable triceps pushdown2 × 10

The row keeps the shoulder balanced as the pressing climbs. The machine and pushdowns add pressing without more barbell work on the shoulder.

B3 Light paused bench 20 min · weekend
  • Shoulder warmup with band pull-aparts5 min
  • Paused bench, 1 s on the chest3 × 5 at T − 25
  • Seated Y raise2 × 5 at 20

Blades pinned. The bar touches and stops; the chest doesn't sink to meet it.

J Jumps and dunk 40 min · weekend
  • Bike or sled5–10 min
  • Pogo hops2 × 20
  • Depth jumps2 × 5
  • Approach jumps to the wall target, one foot and two~10 min
  • Weighted jumps2 × 5 at 20s
  • Bulgarian split squat2 × 6 a leg
  • Lying leg curl2 × 5

A set ends when a rep is slow or a landing gets loud. With no hoop this winter, the approach jumps aim at a strip of tape on a wall; stop them when two touches in a row land 2 inches below the day's best. Keep the one-foot jumps about even between the legs. A hoop, when there is one, replaces the wall.

J2 Short jumps 10 min · before weekday Zone 2
  • The first minutes of the Zone 2 bike, easy5 min
  • Approach jumps to the wall target, one foot and two3 × 3

The week's second jump exposure, three or four days from the weekend session. It rides with the weekday Zone 2 session and drops when that session does.

I Intervals 25 min · once a week
  • Bike: hard enough for only a few words4 × 4 min
  • Easy between3 min

Starts as 4 × 2 min. Swaps: sled pushes; heavy bag 5 × 3 min with wraps and gloves, punching through the bag and never into the air; the rower while the back is quiet. Basketball replaces it in any week you play.

Z Zone 2 ~2 h a week · any 20–60 min chunk
  • Bike or ellipticalfirst choice
  • Treadmill~10% incline
  • Sledwhen it's free

Easy enough to talk in full sentences. A flat walk rarely gets there. Half the amount the first week, the full amount by the end of the third.

Knees and shins 12 min · rides with the jump day
  • Backwards sled, or backwards treadmill walk5 min
  • Wall tibialis raise2 × 20
  • Slow step-up, knee over toe2 × 10 a leg

For the tib raise, stand with your back to a wall, heels a foot out and legs straight, and lift the toes toward the shins. The knees take every landing, and this keeps them up to it as the jumping doubles.

Hips and calves 12 min · rides with the light bench
  • Knee raises on the roman chair2 × 10
  • Slow straight-knee calf raise, leg press or a step2 × 12
  • Reverse hyper on a flat bench2 × 12

The knee raise trains the hip flexors that drive the knee up at takeoff. For the reverse hyper, lie face down with your hips at the end of the bench and raise straight legs until they're level with the body, never higher. It keeps the lower back ready for 300 lbs without loading the spine.

What's cut and what's kept

Everything that stays serves one of the goals, or keeps a joint safe while one of them climbs.

Cut

  • The stretch-under-tension dayServes no goal directly. The loaded-stretch rule still holds for anything that does stretch.
  • Off A and Off B as days of their ownPogos move to the jump day and calf raises to the hips-and-calves block. Copenhagen planks, bird dogs, dead bugs, side planks, pushups and the overhead hold go.
  • Direct ab workAbs show when the fat comes off, and the crunch machine flexes the spine under load. The knee raise stays, for the hip flexors.
  • Bayesian curlIt protected the elbows while pullup volume grew. Pullup volume is now going down.
  • Lat pulldownIts job was building the pullup, which is done.
  • Cable flyA fourth press on the same tissue as bench, dips and the machine.
  • Light trap bar and squat, and the back-off setsOne heavy set a week holds them.
  • Heavy-day jumps, extra pullup sets, the sled finisherThe jump day and the heavy day cover them.

Kept

  • PancakesThe back's warmup before the trap bar.
  • Shoulder warmup and band pull-apartsBefore every bench session.
  • Cable row and Y raiseShoulder balance and insurance while the bench climbs three days a week.
  • Incline machine and triceps pushdownBench volume.
  • Lying leg curlHamstrings for the trap bar and the sprint into an approach jump.
  • Bulgarian split squat and pogosThe one-foot takeoff and the ankles' spring.
  • Backwards sled, tib raise, step-up, calf raise, knee raiseThe shins, calves, knees and hip flexors the jump uses, which nothing else in the week trains.
  • Reverse hyperThe lower back under 300 lbs, without loading the spine.
  • The away routineFor trips only, Mexico included.

Moving things around

No weekday is fixed. These five rules are, and everything else floats.

  1. Five sessions a week at most

    Saturday and Sunday are two of them. Walking doesn't count as a session.

  2. Heavy day once every 5–9 days

    Fed, and never the day after jumps or intervals. On a weekday when one is free; on a weekend, eat first.

  3. A day between bench days

    Heavy, bench volume and light bench all count.

  4. Jumps first and fresh

    First in their session, and never the day after intervals. Both jump sessions count, so the short one never lands the day before the heavy day.

  5. Cardio and joint blocks ride along

    Zone 2 and the two joint blocks attach to any session. They never need a day of their own.

Order within one visit

Fast work, then heavy, then tiring, then easy. A 5–10 minute warmup on the bike or sled is the only exception.

Jumps→ Heavy or bench→ Helpers→ Intervals→ Zone 2→ Joint blocks

How each goal moves

Strength, held

In a deficit, 3 or 4 reps at 300 or 225 on an ordinary week is fine. If a block-end test misses twice, or a top set drops under 3, the cut is too steep: add 150 kcal. If it still slides, a light trap bar goes back onto the jump day.

Bench, climbing

T steps 5 lbs when every heavy-day bench set is clean, and the other two bench days follow it. Expect about 5 lbs a month in the cut and 5 every two weeks at maintenance. If the Nov 19 test comes in higher than 175, every bench number moves up with it.

The cut

  • 2,400 kcal and 170–190 g protein from Nov 30.
  • Aim for 0.75–1 lb a week off the 7-day average.
  • Two flat weeks: cut 150 kcal. Faster than 1.5 lbs a week: add 150.
  • The waist and the mirror decide when it's done, not a number on the scale.

Heart rate

By hand, on waking, before getting up or coffee: a full 60-second count at the wrist. Three mornings a week, into the check-ins. No device needed.

Dunk

With a palm that just about holds, aim for a touch 6–8 inches above the rim, 10'6" to 10'8". With no hoop this winter, the measure is a wall: a strip of tape from 9'6" to 11' with inch marks, touched with chalked fingers. Test it at the end of each block, best of five off one foot and off two. The ladder below waits for a hoop: an outdoor court in spring, or a drop-in.

Is one jump session enough? It would move the jump, but slower. Most programs that raised a vertical used two sessions a week. The second one is ten minutes of approach jumps at the start of the weekday Zone 2 session, three or four days from the weekend session. In a week that loses that session, one jump day is fine. If a hoop turns up in spring, the short jumps become rim practice.

Before November 18

  • Max touch on the wall target. Best of five off one foot and off two. If you get to a hoop once, touch the rim too, so the wall number can be read against it.
  • Resting heart rate. Three mornings this week, by the method above.
  • Waist. Tape at the navel, relaxed, morning. Weekly from here.
  • Weigh-ins on at least four mornings a week. The cut steers by the average.
  • The bench test on Thursday, November 19.

Hypermobility

  • Every rule in Section 7 of the training plan stands. No leg extensions, even though the machine is there.
  • Landings: a set ends when a landing gets loud or a knee drifts in.
  • Heavy bag: wraps and gloves, punch through the bag, never into the air.
  • Rower: intervals only, and only while the back is quiet.
  • Joint blocks: slow, stopping at the first real stretch. Full-depth ATG squats and split squats, Jefferson curls, the couch stretch and elephant walks stay out. The reverse hyper stops at level.
  • Deloads every 4–5 weeks from the BC trip on: same sessions, weights down 10–15%, intervals out.
